What is a HEIC file?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the default photo format used by iPhones and iPads since iOS 11 in 2017. Apple adopted it because HEIC photos are roughly 50% smaller than equivalent JPEGs — meaning your iPhone can store twice as many photos without losing quality.

That's great for iPhone storage. But it creates a problem: Windows doesn't natively support HEIC. When you copy photos from your iPhone to a Windows PC, you get a folder full of .heic files that Windows Photo Viewer, Paint, and most Windows software simply cannot open.

You're not alone — this is one of the most common frustrations for iPhone users on Windows, and it affects millions of people every day.

Why can't Windows open HEIC files?

HEIC is based on the HEIF (High Efficiency Image Format) standard, but it uses Apple's specific implementation. Microsoft added partial support through a paid codec in the Microsoft Store, but:

  • The codec costs money ($0.99 USD)
  • It doesn't always install correctly on all Windows versions
  • Even with the codec, many third-party apps still won't read HEIC
  • It doesn't help if you need to send the photo to someone else who also can't open it

The most practical solution in almost all cases is to simply convert the HEIC file to JPG, which works everywhere.

Method 2: Change your iPhone camera settings to shoot in JPG

If you'd rather prevent the problem than fix it, you can tell your iPhone to save photos as JPG instead of HEIC:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Tap Camera
  3. Tap Formats
  4. Select Most Compatible (instead of High Efficiency)

Your iPhone will now save new photos as JPG. Note that this uses more storage space — roughly twice as much per photo. It also won't help with photos already taken in HEIC format.

Method 3: Buy the HEIC Image Extensions from the Microsoft Store

Microsoft offers a paid HEIF Image Extensions codec in the Microsoft Store for $0.99 USD. Once installed, Windows Photos and some other apps can open HEIC files directly.

Limitations:

  • Costs money
  • Only works in apps that use Windows imaging APIs — many third-party apps still won't open HEIC
  • Doesn't help when sharing photos with others who can't open HEIC
  • Has had installation issues on some Windows 11 configurations

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I open HEIC files on Windows?

Windows does not natively support HEIC without a paid codec. HEIC is Apple's format — it's used by iPhones and iPads but was not built into Windows. The easiest solution is to convert HEIC to JPG using a free browser-based tool.

Does converting HEIC to JPG reduce quality?

Slightly — JPG uses lossy compression, so there is a minor quality reduction. However, at the default quality settings used by most converters, the difference is invisible to the naked eye. For professional printing, consider converting to PNG instead for lossless quality.

Can I convert multiple HEIC files at once?

Can I convert HEIC to PNG instead of JPG?

Yes. Use img-vert's HEIC to PNG converter for lossless conversion that preserves full image quality.
