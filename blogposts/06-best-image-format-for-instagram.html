Quick answer: what image format for Instagram?

  • Photos and product images: JPG at 1080px wide, quality 90+
  • Graphics, text, illustrations: PNG for maximum sharpness
  • WebP: Convert to JPG first — Instagram does not accept WebP from desktop
  • HEIC (iPhone photos): Convert to JPG before uploading from Windows or desktop

How Instagram processes your images

The most important thing to understand about Instagram image quality is this: Instagram re-compresses every image you upload. Regardless of how perfect your original file is, Instagram will apply its own JPEG compression before displaying it to followers.

This has two implications:

  1. Start as high quality as possible — Instagram's compression reduces quality, so begin with the best file you can.
  2. Upload at the exact recommended dimensions — if you upload at the right pixel size, Instagram doesn't have to resize (which adds another layer of quality loss).

Instagram's compression is noticeably heavy on images with fine detail, gradients, and text. This is why many creators find Instagram photos look softer or slightly blurry compared to the original — it's Instagram's encoding, not their camera.

JPG vs PNG for Instagram: which is better?

Use JPG for photos

For photographs — portraits, landscapes, product shots, street photography — upload as JPG. Instagram re-encodes everything as JPEG anyway, and starting with JPG means only one encoding step rather than two (PNG → Instagram's JPEG). This preserves slightly more quality in the final displayed image.

Export at quality 90–95 from your editing software. There's no benefit to quality 100 — the file is larger but Instagram will compress it down anyway.

Use PNG for graphics, text, and illustrations

For graphics with text overlays, illustrations, quote cards, or any image with sharp edges and solid colours, upload as PNG. PNG's lossless quality means Instagram starts with a pixel-perfect original before applying its own compression, resulting in sharper text and cleaner edges in the final post.

This is particularly noticeable with text — a JPG version of a quote graphic often looks slightly fuzzy, while a PNG source gives much sharper text in the final Instagram post.

Can you upload WebP images to Instagram?

On Instagram's mobile app, WebP images from your camera roll generally won't appear or upload correctly. On desktop, Instagram's web upload interface does not accept WebP.

If your image is in WebP format (perhaps it came from a website or was exported by a design tool), convert it to JPG before uploading.

Tips for sharper Instagram photos

  1. Shoot at maximum quality — use your phone's highest resolution setting
  2. Export at 1080px wide — not larger (Instagram resizes it anyway) and not smaller
  3. Use JPG at quality 90–95 for photos, PNG for graphics
  4. Avoid double-compression — don't re-export a JPG that's already been exported; each JPG save adds more compression artefacts
  5. Upload over WiFi — Instagram applies heavier compression when it detects a slow connection
  6. Use the web uploader on desktop for the best quality — some users report slightly better results than the mobile app

Frequently Asked Questions

What image format does Instagram use?

Instagram stores and displays images as JPEG. Regardless of what format you upload, Instagram converts everything to JPEG internally. Uploading as JPG gives you one less conversion step.

Why do my Instagram photos look blurry?

Instagram's compression is aggressive. To minimise quality loss: upload at exactly 1080px wide, use JPG at quality 90+, and make sure you're on a fast WiFi connection when uploading.

Does Instagram accept PNG files?

Yes — Instagram accepts both JPG and PNG uploads. Instagram converts everything to JPEG, but uploading as PNG for graphics and text images results in sharper final quality.

What size should I export Instagram photos from Lightroom/Photoshop?

1080px on the long edge, sRGB colour space, JPG at quality 90–95. For Stories and Reels: 1080×1920px.

Can I upload HEIC photos to Instagram from my iPhone?

On iPhone, Instagram handles HEIC automatically. On Windows or other non-Apple devices, convert HEIC to JPG first using img-vert's free HEIC converter.
